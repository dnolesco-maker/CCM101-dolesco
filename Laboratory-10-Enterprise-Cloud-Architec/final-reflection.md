# Final Reflection: My Journey from Mission 1 to Mission 10

Progressing from Mission 1 to Mission 10 in Cloud Computing gave me a clear, hands-on picture of how modern infrastructure actually runs behind the scenes. Starting out as a Junior Trainee focused on basic Linux commands and core cloud concepts, each subsequent mission pushed me to connect theoretical architecture with real system administration.

Working through the mid-level missions helped me understand the practical trade-offs between different cloud service providers (AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud) as well as the architectural differences between traditional virtualization and containerization. Getting hands-on with Docker, Docker Compose, and persistent storage management showed me how isolated services communicate, share data, and scale cleanly within a multi-tier setup.

Beyond deployment, the operations and security missions reinforced that spinning up a server is only half the job. Configuring firewalls, tightening access permissions, tracking system resource usage, and writing Bash automation scripts taught me how to keep an environment resilient and secure. Seeing how automated tasks and reliable backup routines eliminate repetitive manual work and prevent downtime was one of my biggest takeaways.

For my final mission as an Enterprise Cloud Architect, I brought everything together by designing and deploying a complete multi-tier cloud stack using VirtualBox, Ubuntu Server, and Docker with persistent storage. Troubleshooting configuration conflicts on my own, validating the setup, and documenting the entire architecture tested everything I had practiced throughout the course.

Ultimately, Missions 1 through 10 shifted my perspective: cloud computing isn't just about getting an application online—it is about deliberate planning, hardening security, continuous monitoring, and clean documentation. Every broken container and permission error along the way sharpened my debugging skills. As a BSIT student, this practical foundation in Linux, container orchestration, and infrastructure management gives me real confidence heading into my IT career.
